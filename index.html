Naša pričaOur story

The Little Pantry je porodični biznis koji je osnovala škotsko-srpska porodica u Novom Sadu.

Inspirisani tradicionalnim britanskim receptima i ljubavlju prema kvalitetnim sastojcima, ručno pripremamo domaće proizvode u malim serijama.

Od našeg prepoznatljivog lemon curd-a i marmelade od pomorandže, do granole i škotskog tablet slatkiša – svaki proizvod nastaje sa posebnom pažnjom, posvećenošću i sastojcima u koje verujemo.

The Little Pantry is a family business started by a Scottish-Serbian family in Novi Sad.

Inspired by traditional British recipes and a love of good ingredients, we make homemade goods by hand, in small batches.

From our signature lemon curd and orange marmalade to granola and Scottish tablet, every product is made with care, dedication and ingredients we trust.

Handcrafted with care. Made to be shared.

Naši proizvodiOur products

Lemon Curd

210 g · 720 g

Gladak i puterast namaz pun svežeg, osvežavajućeg limuna. Savršen na tostu, u pecivu ili uz jogurt. Svaka tegla donosi malo sunčanog ukusa u vaš dan!

A smooth, buttery spread full of fresh, zesty lemon. Perfect on toast, in pastries or with yogurt. Every jar brings a little sunshine to your day!

Orange Marmalade

275 g

Klasičan citrusni namaz koji spaja slatke i blago gorke note svežih pomorandži. Odličan na tostu ili uz sir, za malo sofisticiraniji zalogaj.

A classic citrus spread with sweet and gently bitter notes of fresh oranges. Great on toast, or with cheese for a more grown-up bite.

Scottish Tablet

270 g

Pravimo ga od našeg domaćeg kondenzovanog mleka, koje lagano kuvamo pre nego što ga pretvorimo u tablet. Blago mrvičast i veoma sladak.

Made from our own homemade condensed milk, slowly cooked before it becomes tablet. Slightly crumbly and very sweet.

Apple Pie Granola

200 g

Topla i mirisna granola inspirisana pitom od jabuka. Domaći pire od jabuka, bademi, orasi, suvo grožđe, ovas i topli začini.

Warm, fragrant granola inspired by apple pie. Homemade apple purée, almonds, walnuts, raisins, oats and warm spices.

Banana Choco Crunch Granola

200 g

Hrskava mešavina ovsenih pahuljica, banana, komadića tamne čokolade, suncokretovih i lanenih semenki. Bez orašastih plodova.

A crunchy mix of oats, banana, dark chocolate pieces, sunflower and flax seeds. Nut-free.
